Privacy

Effective 23 November 2026 · Česky

Pluffs is a game for children, so we keep only what the game can't work without. No accounts, e-mails, ads or third-party trackers, and we set no cookies.

Who is responsible

The data controller is Ing. Vladimír Skoupý, company ID (IČO) 01227386. For questions, a copy of your data or to have it deleted, write to hello@pluffs.cz.

Stored on your device

  • A profile key: a random secret code the game recognises you by.
  • Settings: nickname, chosen map, sound and music, language, and which tips you've seen.

These are in your browser's storage (localStorage); clearing pluffs.cz's site data removes them.

Stored on our server

  • Your profile: a random ID, a recovery code (to play on another device), your look and your collection (which parts you have, how many and since when).
  • Buttons: a record of what you earned and when, and what you spent them on (matches, set purchases).
  • Key fingerprints: only a hash of your device's key, never the key itself.
  • One-time move codes for moving to the game's new address: valid 5 minutes, then deleted.
  • Error reports: when the game crashes or the server refuses a request, it tells us what failed, the game version, the page and the browser type. Nothing about you. Deleted after 30 days.

Not stored

  • Your nickname is only seen by players in the same online room while you play; we don't store it. Use a nickname, not your real name.
  • Your IP address isn't recorded by us. Our hosting provider briefly processes it to deliver the game and protect it from floods.

Why and for how long

We need this data for the game to work: to keep your collection and Buttons, to let you play on several devices, and to fix bugs (legitimate interest and providing the service). Profiles are kept while you use the game or until you ask us to delete them; error reports for 30 days.

Who processes data for us

The game runs on Cloudflare, Inc. (servers, database, delivery), which processes data only on our behalf, under contractual safeguards for transfers outside the EU.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, to correct or delete it, and object to its use. Write to hello@pluffs.cz with your recovery code (in the Wardrobe, under the key) so we know which profile it is. You can also complain to the Czech data protection authority, ÚOOÚ.

For parents

The game asks for no personal data and has no ads and no real-money purchases. If that changes, we'll update this page before it takes effect.
